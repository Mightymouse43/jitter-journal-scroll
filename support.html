02 / Subscriptions

MANAGING YOUR SUBSCRIPTION

To cancel or manage your Jitter Journal Premium subscription, go to your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and select Jitter Journal.

You can cancel at any time. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. We do not offer refunds for partial periods, but Apple may issue refunds at their discretion — contact Apple Support for refund requests.

03 / Common Issues

TROUBLESHOOTING

  • AI roast not loading: Make sure you have an active internet connection. If the issue persists, force-quit and reopen the app.
  • Camera not working: Go to Settings → Jitter Journal and make sure Camera access is enabled.
  • Premium features not unlocked after purchase: Tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall screen to sync your subscription.
  • Log entries not saving: Ensure you're signed in. Go to Settings → Jitter Journal and check that the app has not been restricted.
